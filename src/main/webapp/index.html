Official Tariff Slabs

Charges are applied progressively across slab thresholds, never a single rate for all units.

  • Slab 1
    First 50 units (0 – 50) ₹3.50 / unit
  • Slab 2
    Next 100 units (51 – 150) ₹4.00 / unit
  • Slab 3
    Next 100 units (151 – 250) ₹5.20 / unit
  • Slab 4
    Above 250 units ₹6.50 / unit
Example (300 Units)
50 × ₹3.50 = ₹175
100 × ₹4.00 = ₹400
100 × ₹5.20 = ₹520
50 × ₹6.50 = ₹325
Total = ₹1,420.00
